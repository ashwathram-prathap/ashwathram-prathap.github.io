~/llm-engineLLM Inference Engine

CS Built with Codex · 2026

What it is
A large language model inference engine built from scratch in C++20 and Apple Metal on my MacBook, by directing Codex AI coding agents. It runs GPT-2 and Qwen2.5 (0.5B and 7B), with a BPE tokenizer, KV cache, grouped-query attention and 4-bit quantization.
Results
Runs Qwen2.5-7B in 4-bit at 22.3 tokens/s in 4.09 GiB, about 82% of llama.cpp's speed on byte-identical weights. A 4-bit GPU kernel fix raised weight bandwidth from 29 to 97 GB/s for 3.1x faster decode, with logits matching Hugging Face.
  • C++20
  • Apple Metal
  • Codex

~/balancing-cubeReaction-Wheel Balancing Cube

CS With a partner · In Progress

What it does
A cube that balances on its corner using three reaction wheels, as a first step toward building a UAV from scratch.

First prototype built in 4 days. Now porting the control to an STM32F411 with a hardware-timed loop (a 500 Hz attitude filter and a 200 Hz state-feedback controller) while redesigning the flywheel and brackets and learning PCB design.
